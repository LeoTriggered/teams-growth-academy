A Growing Library

The T.E.A.M.S. Coaching Growth Academy is a comprehensive student development platform with 29 Digital Labs organized into 5 T.E.A.M.S. Series—Transition, Empowerment, Athletic Mindset, Management, and Social.

Labs are released in installments throughout the year, allowing every Partner School's library to continually expand with fresh content and resources.

T.E.A.M.S. Series

Transition Series · 6 Labs

Navigating New Environments · Middle School · High School · Pathways After High School · Discipline & Responsibility · Independent Living

Empowerment Series · 6 Labs

Peer Pressure · Self-Esteem · Finding Your Voice · Values · Identity · Purpose

Athletic Mindset Series · 5 Labs

Maximize Value · Sportsmanship · Athletic Leadership · Expectations · Identity Beyond Athletics

Management Series · 7 Labs

Financial Literacy · Earning Your Keep · Jobs · Time · Budget · Hygiene · Stress & Adversity

Social Series · 5 Labs

Healthy Relationships · Effective Communication · Reading the Room · Filter · Etiquette

Every Partner School receives access to newly released Series Labs throughout their active partnership.
