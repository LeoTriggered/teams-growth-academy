Built for parents

You do not have to wait for them to sit down.

Most programs for teens give parents one more chore: get your kid to sit down. This one starts with you.

You watch the Parent Lab. In one sitting you learn what they are up against, what helps, and what to say. You can use it that night, even if they never press play.
