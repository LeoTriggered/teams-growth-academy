Meet Coach Walker

For more than 20 years, Coach Walker has dedicated his career to helping young people build confidence, develop character, strengthen relationships, and successfully navigate life’s biggest transitions.

As a teacher, coach, principal, mentor, and parent, he has worked alongside thousands of students and families, gaining firsthand insight into the challenges today’s teens face.

Through T.E.A.M.S. Coaching, Coach Walker has helped students improve communication, build leadership skills, develop healthy habits, strengthen executive functioning, increase motivation, and create meaningful goals for their futures. Every lesson, coaching strategy, and activity has been shaped by real conversations, real experiences, and real results.

Today, Coach Walker is excited to bring the most impactful elements of the T.E.A.M.S. Coaching mentorship program into a flexible digital learning experience that families can access anywhere.
