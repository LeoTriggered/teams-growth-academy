The T.E.A.M.S. Coaching Growth Academy helps students develop the life skills that prepare them for success in school, relationships, careers, and life.

Created by Coach Walker, an educator, administrator, mentor, and coach with more than 20 years of experience, the Growth Academy teaches students how to communicate effectively, become confident leaders, build healthy relationships, navigate life’s transitions, and discover purpose—all at their own pace.

Whether you’re a homeschool family, homeschool co-op, microschool, or hybrid learning program, the Growth Academy is designed to complement your existing effective curriculum.
